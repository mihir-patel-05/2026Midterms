What is the War Room?

A command center for the midterms, built for people who follow the count.

Most election sites show you one thing: the results map, or the odds, or the news. The War Room keeps them together. Pick the races you care about, and every screen follows your watchlist, from the poll closing timeline to the race dossiers to the alerts that fire when something gets called.

It runs in the browser, it is free, and it is built to keep working through the busiest hour of the night.

What's inside

Eleven modules. One watchlist ties them together.

NIGHT

Election night

Poll closing timeline, your key races, call alerts, live coverage and links to every state's official results.

 CMD

Command

Chamber control, key figures, the intel feed and your watchlist on one board.

Call alerts

An alert pops up the moment a race on your watchlist is called, and if a call is ever taken back, the War Room flags that too.

Control called

A banner goes up as soon as a party clinches the House or the Senate, counting the vice president's tiebreak.

Next close countdown

The header always shows which states close next and how long until they do.

Runoffs and ranked choice

Georgia's runoff rule and ranked-choice voting in Maine and Alaska are marked, so a leader under 50% isn't mistaken for a winner.

Honest about stale data

If the market feed drops, prices are marked stale with the time they were last live, and the feed reconnects on its own.

FAQ

Questions.

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no paywall, no trial.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The War Room is built for phones as well as big screens, and on iPhone or iPad you can add it to your home screen like an app.

Are these the official results?

No. Results are unofficial until each state certifies them. The Night screen links straight to each state's election office for the official count.

Why show betting markets?

Markets react within seconds when new votes come in, so they are a fast signal of what people who follow the count expect.

Who makes this?

The War Room is part of VoteInformed, an nonpartisan civic project that helps people follow their elections.
